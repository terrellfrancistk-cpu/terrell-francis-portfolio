TK HUB — WHAT TERRELL WANTS THE WHOLE HOUSE TO BE

1. The vision
TK Hub is one house with many different businesses. Terrell Francis owns it through TK Hub LLC. The house exists to make money from real products, not from a pile of templates that look like one app reskinned.

The order of value he already set, most cash first:
- TK Works and TK Dental are services. They sell now.
- Apex is the product he expects to make the most money. A clinic pays every month. Get it demo-ready before any vendor pitch.
- Booked keeps the fee that Thumbtack takes. No lead fee.
- Curb is the DFW ride and food marketplace. Goal he set: 10,000 riders and a driver share that is visible before the car moves.
- Zilt is the attention business. Post, play, get paid.
- NOIR is the long session, virtual gold, 18+.
- Afterglow is nights and standing. Not a casino.
- TORQ is the racing game, phone and then the same game on his Razer in Unreal, then consoles. It was called WRACE. The name on screen is TORQ.
- Our People is the family circle.
- RADR is road intel and navigation.
- 4AM Files is files after dark. It is not Air and it is not NOIR.
- TORQ on console is the same game, later. Not a second game.

He wants every competitor's feature, done, and done better, plus the extra he named. A menu that opens a blank page does not count. Demo people on a live board do not count. A button that sends him to a Rork address does not count.

He tests on his phone. He presses Publish. Builders edit GitHub. They do not type in a Rork chat, do not press "Fix with Rork," and do not spend Rork credits. If a frame is empty, say it is empty.

3. The quality bar
He has rejected the same failures more than once. This is the bar.

- The first screen after a successful sign-in is the product. Not a counter. Not "you're in, and counted." Not a create-account page that appears after he is already in.
- Every button does the thing it says. If it cannot, the control is disabled and one sentence says why. It does not throw, spin forever, or open a dead host.
- Demo off is empty of sample people, sample pros, sample matches, and sample jobs. Demo on is labeled and asks first. Turning it off does not wipe the real save.
- A control looks like its state. Off is off. A knob stays inside the track.
- Light, Dark, and System exist. System is the default and follows the phone or the browser. The app's brand colors are the skin inside those modes. Signing in does not change the theme.
- Inside one app, every tab looks like that app. A tab that jumps to a different design is a bug.
- Targets are big enough to hit. Games that are landscape stay landscape. No "hold sideways" wall.
- Motion is part of the product. A walker walks. A video plays. A lever that exists moves. A wheel that exists turns. A car is a car from eight feet, not a box.
- Errors are one plain sentence. No stack trace. No "something slipped" with no way back.
- Empty is allowed. Lying is not.
- He should never have to do a console step twice. If a subscription, a URL, a brand, or a key already exists, do not create another.
- Version numbers are his. 1.9.6 stays unless he types a new one. Changing a number is not changing the code, and changing the code is not an excuse to change the number.
