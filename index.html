Selected work

Case studies

Problem, approach, and result, drawn from my work at Wellfit Technologies and MB2 Dental. Client names are kept confidential.

01

Wellfit Technologies · Implementation Supervisor · Process improvement

Turning a fragmented implementation process into a repeatable playbook

~85%shorter cycle time

Problem

The implementation process was fragmented. Launches were effectively reinvented each time, and the process itself was slowing customers' path to go-live while the team carried a large concurrent workload.

Approach

Rebuilt delivery into a standard, repeatable playbook backed by templates, checklists, and trackers. Trained my two-person implementation team on it, and kept the most complex client projects and escalations myself.

Result

Implementation cycle time dropped by ~85%, and launches followed one consistent, documented path instead of a custom effort every time.

02

Wellfit Technologies · Healthcare fintech SaaS · Implementation & onboarding

Running 85+ concurrent implementations as the primary client contact

85+concurrent implementations

Problem

Each new account, typically a healthcare practice of ~10–50 employees, had to go from signed contract to live with its own workflows, staff, and devices, while dozens of other accounts were in flight at the same time.

Approach

Owned each account as the primary point of contact through discovery, configuration, training, and go-live. Configured software and connected devices around each client's real business process, and kept client leadership plus internal Product, Engineering, Support, and Sales aligned on goals, risks, dates, and decisions.

Result

85+ concurrent implementations carried, each typically ~1 month from kickoff to go-live, with accounts launching on workflows their staff already understood.

03

MB2 Dental · IT Field Technician / Systems Administrator · Deployment

Delivering an ~800-device hardware and software go-live

~800devices live

Problem

A large hardware and software rollout inside a dental support organization serving 700+ locations, in live environments where any downtime hit operations immediately.

Approach

Ran the deployment with serial tracking and remote installation, coordinated vendors, site leaders, and internal teams, and trained clinical and administrative staff through the change.

Result

An ~800-device go-live with every device accounted for and configured consistently. The configurations and procedures I documented made later implementations faster and cleaner.

04

Wellfit Technologies · Customer success · Adoption & handoff

Go-lives that stick: training to independence and clean Support handoffs

✓documented, client-owned accounts

Problem

A go-live isn't finished if the client still depends on the implementation team to operate, or if Support inherits an account with no documentation behind it.

Approach

Owned setup, end-user training, and the help-center handoff for every account I implemented, so the path from implementation to ongoing support was planned rather than improvised.

Result

Clients ran the product independently after go-live, and Support inherited documented, clean accounts.
