Case studies

Problem, approach, and result, where software meets real operations.

From 700+ MB2 Dental locations to Wellfit accounts I took live. Client names kept confidential.

01

Wellfit Technologies · Implementation Manager · Process improvement

Turning a fragmented implementation process into a repeatable playbook

~85%shorter cycle time

Problem

Every launch was reinvented, slowing the path to go-live under a heavy concurrent workload.

Approach

Rebuilt delivery into one playbook backed by templates, checklists, and trackers.

Trained my two-person team on it, and kept the hardest projects and escalations myself.

Result

Cycle time dropped ~85%. Every launch followed one documented path, not a custom effort.

02

Wellfit Technologies · Healthcare fintech SaaS · Implementation & onboarding

Running 85+ concurrent implementations as the primary client contact

85+concurrent implementations

Problem

Each new account, typically a practice of ~10–50 employees, had its own workflows, staff, and devices.

Dozens of other accounts were in flight at the same time.

Approach

Owned each account as primary contact through discovery, configuration, training, and go-live.

Configured software and devices around each client's real business process.

Kept client leadership and Product, Engineering, Support, and Sales aligned on goals, risks, and dates.

Result

85+ concurrent implementations, each about 1 month from kickoff to go-live, on workflows staff knew.

03

~800devices live

Approach

04

Wellfit Technologies · Customer success · Adoption & handoff

Go-lives that stick: training to independence and clean Support handoffs

✓documented, client-owned accounts
